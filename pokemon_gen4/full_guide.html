Generation 4 - Living Pokédex Guide

Goal and guidelines

The goal of this guide is to help anyone complete a Living Pokédex in the Gen 4 games (Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver). Since one of each Pokémon has to be owned at the same time, planning is essential. Here are the directions followed when writing it:

  • This guide is meant to be used as a template, not a rigid step-by-step walkthrough. The Pokémon info page is here to help adapting it to your own playstyle.
  • Pokémon Platinum is chosen as the main Sinnoh game, as its Pokédex is larger and its extras (the Distortion World, the Battle Zone, both orbs) make it the most complete version. Diamond and Pearl additions are pointed out along the way.
  • HeartGold is chosen for Johto and Kanto, with SoulSilver differences listed on the Pokémon info page (Groudon is SoulSilver only, Kyogre is HeartGold only, and so on).
  • All five Gen 4 games can trade with each other directly, which makes moving Pokémon around much easier than in Gen 3.
  • The seven Pokémon absent from Gen 4 games (Mew, Celebi, the three Regis, Jirachi and Deoxys) must be transferred with the Pal Park from Gen 3 games. The Gen 3 guide explains how to obtain every one of them, event Pokémon included.
  • Access to in-game events is assumed for Darkrai, Shaymin, Arceus and Manaphy (see the extra info page).

Additional information

For the smoothest experience possible, here are some extra details on how to read and use the Living Pokédex guide:

  • Within each area, Pokémon are loosely ordered by the order they become reachable in. Rare Pokémon should be prioritized, as common ones will be encountered naturally.
  • Every evolution gets its own entry: catching the pre-evolution is described once, and the evolution entry tells you what to do with another instance of it. Breeding is the easiest source of extra instances.
  • This checklist and the Pokédex tracker are linked. If a Pokémon is caught in one page, refresh the other page to see the change.
  • The Poké Radar, Honey trees, daily swarms and GBA slot encounters have their own sections, as they require specific setups.
  • The catch locations page details every encounter table of the five games, including the Safari Zone objects and the radio songs.
  • More information about Gen 4 and general tips are available on the extra info page.
